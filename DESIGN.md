# DESIGN.md — NITIP DI END

## Identity
Personal jastip storefront for the owner's wife. Brand voice: bold, warm, direct Indonesian ("EH, NITIP DONG!"). Not a generic marketplace.

## Personality
Neo-brutalist: thick ink borders, hard offset shadows, cream paper, single yellow accent. Confident and playful, never soft-SaaS or purple-glow.

## Palette
- Paper: `#FAF6EF`
- Ink: `#141414`
- Card: `#FFFFFF`
- Accent (money / CTA only): `#FFC800`
- Muted text: `#475569` (AA on paper)
- Dark paper: `#121212` / card `#1E1E1E`

## Typography
- Display / headings: **Bricolage Grotesque** (chunky neo-brutal character)
- Body / UI: **DM Sans** (readable product lists and forms)
- Weights: black/extrabold for brand moments; medium for supporting copy
- Numbers: tabular (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`)

## Motif
Yellow marker underline (`.nb-marker`), 2px ink border + 3–4px hard shadow on interactive surfaces. Accent color reserved for CTAs and money moments.

## Layout
Content-first storefront: hero (brand + CTA), categories, featured / popular / viral catalogs, WhatsApp help. No fake testimonials or invented stats.

## Dial
ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

- ENERGY 2: bold type and hard geometry, not scream-everywhere
- RHYTHM 2: section compositions vary (hero band, grid, inverted viral strip, help panel)
- MOTION 2: button press, card hover lift, scroll-reveal, cart badge pulse; no endless decorative loops

## Audience
Indonesian shoppers titiping lifestyle goods through a personal jastip they trust.
